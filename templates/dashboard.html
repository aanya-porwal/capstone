New to investing? Plain-English guide
Profit
What your money earns on top of what you put in.
ETF / Mutual fund
A ready-made basket of investments, so you don't have to pick individual companies.
Lock-in
A period during which you cannot take your money out.
Why not put everything in one place?
Spreading money across different kinds of investments means one bad patch doesn't hurt your whole plan.
Why does the time matter?
The longer you can wait, the more risk you can take, because markets have time to recover from dips.
